# OctoAcme Project Management

Welcome to OctoAcme's project-management process documentation. These guides help cross-functional teams deliver product features, services, and integrations with clear ownership, iterative delivery, and data-informed decisions. They are for Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, stakeholders, and anyone contributing to a project.

## Project lifecycle

1. **Initiation** validates the business need, success metrics, stakeholders, resources, and an initial timeline before a go/no-go decision.
2. **Planning** turns an approved initiative into shippable increments, a prioritized backlog, milestones, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and a Definition of Done.
3. **Execution and tracking** uses project boards, pull requests, testing, demos, and metrics to build, review, test, and iterate toward milestones.
4. **Risk management and communication** run throughout planning and delivery through a maintained Risk Register, regular status updates, and defined escalation paths.
5. **Release and deployment** prepares release notes, rollback plans, and smoke tests; deploys and verifies production changes; then communicates the release.
6. **Retrospective and continuous improvement** captures learning after sprints, releases, milestones, and incidents, then tracks owned action items.

## Roles, artifacts, and cadence

- **Project Managers** coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, documentation, and communication.
- **Product Managers** define outcomes and success metrics, prioritize the roadmap and backlog, and measure impact.
- **Developers** design, implement, test, and review work while identifying technical risks.
- **QA/Testing** validates quality and acceptance criteria.
- **Stakeholders** provide input, decisions, and approvals.

The process is supported by the Project One-pager, prioritized Sprint/Iteration Backlog, Acceptance Criteria and Definition of Done, Roadmap and Release Plan, Risk Register, and retrospective notes with action items. Teams maintain momentum through daily standups, weekly delivery and PM/Product Manager syncs, sprint or milestone demos, regular stakeholder updates, and retrospectives.
